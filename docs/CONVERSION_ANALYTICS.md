# ARIKARTECH — Conversion Analytics & Funnel Optimization

## 1. Funnel Architecture
The conversion funnel is tracked end-to-end without collecting PII:

$$\text{Top of Funnel (Search \& Discovery)} \longrightarrow \text{Product View} \longrightarrow \text{Offer View} \longrightarrow \text{Affiliate Outbound Click}$$

$$\text{Referral CTR} = \left( \frac{\text{Outbound Affiliate Clicks}}{\text{Total Search \& Discovery Interactions}} \right) \times 100$$

---

## 2. Multi-Dimensional Attribution Breakdowns
The `/admin/affiliates/performance` dashboard breaks down performance across:
1. **Network Attribution**: Performance across CJ Affiliate, Awin, Impact, and Direct Retailers.
2. **Merchant Retailer**: Identifying which retailers have the highest click-through rates.
3. **Country Market**: Tracking revenue demand across US, UK, DE, FR, ES, IT, NL, AU, and NZ.
4. **Top Products**: Identifying high-converting hardware models.
