---

## 2. Dynamic Schema.org Implementations
1. **Product & AggregateOffer**:
   - Materialized with valid ISO currencies (`USD`, `GBP`, `EUR`, `AUD`, `NZD`).
   - Availability mapped to Schema.org schema enums (`https://schema.org/InStock`, `https://schema.org/OutOfStock`).
2. **BreadcrumbList**:
   - Generates nested hierarchical schema (`Home` > `Category` > `Product`).
3. **WebSite & SearchAction**:
   - Powers Google Sitelinks Search Box.

---

## 3. Google Search Console & Sitemap Submission
1. **Verification**: Verify domain ownership using DNS TXT record or HTML tag in `<head>`.
2. **Sitemap Index Submission**: Submit `https://arikartech.com/sitemap.xml`.
3. **International Targeting**: Ensure geographic targeting aligns with `hreflang` headers for all 9 markets.
