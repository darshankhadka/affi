## 2. Programmatic SEO Quality Guard

To protect search engine crawl quality and domain reputation across all 35 markets:

1. **Eligible for Indexation (`index, follow`)**:
   - Product is `published`.
   - Has a quality score >= 40 points.
   - Has at least one valid, active commercial offer in the target market.

2. **Indexation Protected (`noindex, follow`)**:
   - Market page with zero active store offers.
   - Stale or expired prices.
   - Prevents thin content penalties across regional landing pages.
